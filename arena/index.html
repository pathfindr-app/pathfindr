PATHFINDR / LOCAL EXPERIMENT

New Cairo
Circuit.

Five separate S → E routes. Four players, each on a different pair. Your route is cyan.

  1. Draw, just like Classic. Tap streets or trace directly. Retrace to erase; Undo removes your last section. Desktop: WASD or arrow keys also trace. Drag to pan; pinch to zoom.
  2. Finish your own route. Reach your E, then receive another fixed S → E pair. Everyone completes all five; no pair is assigned to two players at once. If your remaining pairs are occupied, wait for one to open.
  3. Collect and disrupt. Nearby diamonds grant charge. Barrier closes safe streets for 10 seconds. Cut requires a rival to reconnect. Shortcut bridges up to 220 m.

First to complete all five wins. Others get 25 seconds to finish. Eight-minute limit. Local bots only; no account or leaderboard submission.
